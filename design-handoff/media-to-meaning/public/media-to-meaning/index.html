Stage 05How does a story move?Roger

Roger widens the aperturePerfusion.

The question grows past “how much was something discussed?” Where did a story start? Who picked it up, who amplified it, and how did the framing change on the way? Did social media lead television or follow it? Did U.S. coverage get rebroadcast abroad, and come back altered?

“How is a story perfused? Where did it originate from? How does it get perfused through a media landscape?”

FrameworkRef. 07

Tolerance for ambiguity

Introduced by Roger

IlluminatesHow differently people handle uncertainty, unresolved questions and changing conditions. A bridge between information design and political psychology.

Uncertainty ↔ certainty → how people read evidence
On the path · Stage 10

Some people are comfortable with uncertainty, open questions and competing explanations. Others strongly prefer certainty, stable categories and clear rules. Roger’s point: tools meant to build new kinds of consensus have to account for both.

Concept unlockedRef. 08

Paradox of tolerance

Introduced by Tom

IlluminatesThe limits of pluralism: how a tolerant system handles beliefs that are themselves intolerant.

Open system → intolerant actors → limits of tolerance
On the path · Stage 10

The idea: if a society extends unlimited tolerance even to people who would abolish tolerance, the intolerant can use that openness to destroy it, and tolerance disappears with it. So an open society may need some limit on tolerating the intolerant in order to remain open.

Formulated by the philosopher Karl Popper in The Open Society and Its Enemies (1945). Shown as the concept Tom raised, not as a conclusion of this piece.

Tom“What are the rules of this game we’re playing? … We can’t even agree on the rules of the game… so then it’s just chaos, and no one wants to live in chaos.”
